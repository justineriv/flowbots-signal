The Investment

What Does It Cost?

Typical project range $15,000 – $300,000

Custom AI automation projects typically range from $15,000 to $300,000, depending on scope and complexity.

Scope

Number of processes automated. More processes means more integrations, testing, and custom logic — which increases the investment.

Complexity

System integrations required. Connecting to legacy systems, custom APIs, or multi-step decision logic takes more engineering time.

Industry Requirements

Regulated industries need additional security, compliance layers, audit trails, and specialized data handling protocols.
