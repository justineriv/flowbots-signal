How AI Handles Patient Calls

FlowBots.ai voice AI answers inbound patient calls with a natural-sounding conversational agent trained on your practice's specific scheduling rules, provider availability, insurance panels, and appointment types.

After-hours calls represent one of the biggest revenue leaks in medical and dental practices. A patient with a toothache at 8 PM is ready to book right now. An answering service takes a message. FlowBots.ai dental AI books the appointment.
